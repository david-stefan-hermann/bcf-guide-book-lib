# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- The inventory key (E by default) closes the book, as it closes the inventory.
- No more welcome gift: with several mods installed a new world started with a pile of books. `Book.giveOnJoin()` and `Book.giveOnce()` stay and do nothing, so mods built against 1.0 keep running.

## 1.0.0
- The guide book of World Radio and Greek Trees as a library: one screen with tabs on both sides, pages that break themselves, recipes read from the mod's own files, the book as an item and as a welcome gift, colours per mod.
